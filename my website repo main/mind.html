FIRST : MIND LOOP REGENRATION OF REALITY

Memory Constructivism is a concept stating that every time you recall a memory, you are not playing a file—you are making a copy of the original.



The human brain does not store memories like a video tape. Instead, it collects a few key structural details and focuses heavily on how you felt, rather than recording a raw image or video. Every time you remember an event, you are actively editing the file. Your brain builds the memory entirely from scratch, piecing it together using logic, present-day emotions, and your pre-established mental schemas (your internal model of the world).



This leads to a scary realization: the emotions you feel right now (sadness, regret, happiness) are triggered by a fabricated copy. The original truth is completely gone.





The Theory of Mind: Reality as a Copy of a Copy



If we extend this concept to our entire theory of mind, we arrive at a terrifying conclusion.



My theory is simple: Every time we interact with reality—physical spaces, people, objects—our mind loops and renders that reality based entirely on our internal model. It filters what we see through our logic, current emotions, beliefs, biases, and habits. Because of this constant feedback loop, you are living in a copy of a copy of a copy... endlessly repeated.



Eventually, we land on a version of existence so deeply fabricated and disjointed from actual objective reality that it is no longer even real.





The Parallel: AI Model Collapse



We are seeing this exact phenomenon play out right now in artificial intelligence, known as AI Model Collapse. If you give a generative model an image or a piece of text and ask it to continuously reproduce it ten times over, it eventually produces something utterly bizarre and disconnected from the original input.



Why? Because with every single generation, the AI introduces tiny pixel pattern anomalies, its own mathematical bias, and its inner logic. These microscopic errors accumulate over time, ending in a completely disjointed final product. Our brains do the exact same thing.





The Age Distortion



We can draw one final, haunting conclusion from this theory.



The younger we are, the more we experience and comprehend reality exactly as it is. It might not be a 100% perfect translation, but it is close to the raw data. However, as we get older, our mental filters calcify. We become deeply disjointed, trapping ourselves inside a false, fictionalized, and illusioned copy of reality—one that does not even exist.
